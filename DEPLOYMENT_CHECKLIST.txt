COMMON ISSUES & SOLUTIONS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ISSUE: Google Ads approval rejected with "Unverified Business"
SOLUTION:
- Ensure company address is visible on About page
- Add business registration details to Terms
- Add contact email and support hours to Contact page
- Resubmit after 24 hours

ISSUE: Google Ads approval rejected with "Destination Not Working"
SOLUTION:
- Test all links on your site
- Ensure 404.html is properly configured
- Check .htaccess file for URL rewriting issues
- Test with: curl -H "User-Agent: Googlebot" https://fanliteplay.com

ISSUE: Google Ads approval rejected with "Gambling/RMG Policy Violation"
SOLUTION:
- Remove any words: "bet", "wager", "odds", "prize pool", "cash", "money"
- Add explicit RMG disclaimer to footer
- Ensure "100% Free" is visible on every page
- Remove any monetary symbols (₹, $)
- Resubmit after 48 hours

ISSUE: Googlebot can't crawl my site
SOLUTION:
- Check Cloudflare WAF rules - whitelist Google bots
- Check robots.txt - ensure it allows /
- Check .htaccess - ensure it doesn't block bots
- Test: curl -H "User-Agent: Googlebot" https://fanliteplay.com/robots.txt
