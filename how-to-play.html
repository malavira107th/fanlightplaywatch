Get Started

How to Play

Everything you need to know to get started on FanLite Play — India's new world of free cricket entertainment. Simple, social, and 100% free.

Your Journey
5 Simple Steps to Get Started
1
Step One

Create Your Free Account

Sign up on FanLite Play in under 60 seconds. All you need is a valid email address and a username. No payment details, no credit card, no deposits — ever. Your account is completely free to create and free to use.

  • Enter your name, email, and choose a username
  • Verify your email address
  • Set up your profile and you are ready to go
2
Step Two

Browse Upcoming Contests

Explore all upcoming cricket matches and entertainment contests available on the platform. Each contest shows you the match details, number of participants, and the leaderboard structure. Browse freely — there is no cost to look around.

  • View all live and upcoming cricket matches
  • Filter by match type — Test, ODI, T20
  • See how many users have already joined each contest
3
Step Three

Pick Your Playing XI & Join

Select your 11 players from the available squad for the match. Use player statistics, recent form, and match conditions to make your selections. Assign a Captain and Vice-Captain for bonus points. Once you are happy with your lineup, join the contest — completely free.

  • Choose from Batters, Bowlers, All-Rounders, and Wicket-Keepers
  • Follow the selection rules (minimum players per category)
  • Assign Captain (2x points multiplier) and Vice-Captain (1.5x multiplier)
  • You can create and save multiple lineups per match
4
Step Four

Track Live Scores & Points

Once the match begins, watch your players earn points in real time based on their on-field performance. Every run, wicket, catch, and milestone contributes to your total score. Follow the live match feed and see how your lineup stacks up against other participants.

  • Real-time point updates as the match progresses
  • Live leaderboard showing your current rank
  • Detailed breakdown of points per player
  • Match commentary and key highlights
5
Step Five

Climb the Leaderboard & Earn Glory

After the match ends, final scores are tallied and the leaderboard is updated. Climb the ranks, earn badges and achievements, and build your reputation as the ultimate cricket entertainment enthusiast on FanLite Play. All glory, all free.

  • Contest leaderboard — ranked within a specific match contest
  • Global leaderboard — ranked across all users on the platform
  • Seasonal leaderboard — ranked across an entire cricket season
  • Earn exclusive badges and achievement milestones

Important Note on Points

All points on FanLite Play are purely for entertainment and leaderboard ranking purposes. They carry absolutely no monetary value and cannot be redeemed, transferred, or exchanged for any goods, services, or prizes of any kind. This is a 100% free entertainment platform.

Platform Rules
Key Rules to Know
🔒

Lineup Lock Time

Your lineup is locked at the official match start time. No changes can be made after the toss or match begins.

👥

Selection Rules

Your Playing XI must include at least 1 Wicket-Keeper, 3 Batters, 3 Bowlers, and 1 All-Rounder. Maximum 7 players from one team.

🏆

One Account Only

Each user is permitted one account only. Multiple accounts are strictly prohibited and will result in permanent suspension.

📋

Multiple Lineups

You may create and enter multiple different lineups per match contest to diversify your engagement.

⚠️

Match Cancellation

If a match is cancelled, abandoned, or reduced to fewer than 10 overs per side, the contest may be voided.

🎯

Fair Play

Any attempt to manipulate scores, use bots, or exploit platform bugs will result in immediate account termination.
