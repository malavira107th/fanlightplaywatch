About FanLite Play

We are an Indian platform, proudly built for Indian fans — bringing the pure joy of cricket back to the people who love it most.

Our Story

Bringing the Game Back to the Fans

Cricket in India is not just a sport — it is an emotion. It is the sound of a crowd erupting when a six clears the boundary. It is the argument between friends about who should have been captain. It is the family gathered around a screen, living every ball together.

But in recent years, the joy of the game began to be overshadowed by high-risk apps that turned cricket into a financial gamble. We watched as the pure thrill of the sport became tangled with anxiety, risk, and financial pressure. We wanted to change that.

FanLite Play was created to bring the pure joy of the game back to the fans. We built a platform where friends, families, and communities can come together to discuss, debate, and celebrate live matches — without any financial risk whatsoever.

We believe that the best victories are the ones shared with your crew, and the only thing you should ever risk losing is leaderboard glory. FanLite Play is, and always will be, a 100% free social entertainment platform.

India's New World of Entertainment 2026

Our Mission

To Bring Friends & Families Together

Our mission is simple: to create a completely free, risk-free social environment where every cricket fan in India can experience the full thrill of the game — together with the people they love. No financial pressure. No risk. Just the pure, unfiltered joy of cricket and community.
